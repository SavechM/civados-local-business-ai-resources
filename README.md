## How to Use These Resources

### Using the AI Prompts

1. Copy the prompt you want
2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI tool
3. Fill in the [BRACKETS] with your business details
4. Edit the output to match your voice
5. Use it

### Using the GHL Templates

1. Open the template file
2. Follow the step-by-step setup guide inside GHL
3. Test with a dummy contact before going live
4. Turn on

No developer needed. No coding. No API keys.

## What Is GEO / AEO and Why Does It Matter for Local Businesses?

**GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** is the practice of making your business show up when AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity answer questions about your industry.

**AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)** is optimizing your content so it gets pulled into featured snippets and direct answers on Google.

Together, they determine whether AI recommends *you* or your competitor when someone asks: *"What's the best [plumber / accountant / gym] near me?"*

The businesses that win the next 5 years are not the ones with the most ads. They're the ones AI systems trust and cite.

This repo is part of Civados's effort to make that accessible to every local business owner.
