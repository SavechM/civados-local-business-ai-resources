# AI Prompts: Social Media for Local Businesses

> Consistent social presence signals trust to both Google and AI systems. These prompts help you create a week of content in under 30 minutes.

---

## Prompt 1 — Weekly Content Plan (30 Minutes, 5 Posts)

```
Create a 5-post social media plan for a [BUSINESS TYPE] for the week of [DATE/WEEK].

Business details:
- Business name: [NAME]
- Location: [CITY/REGION]
- Services: [LIST 2-3 MAIN SERVICES]
- Target customer: [e.g. homeowners, small business owners, parents]
- Tone: [professional / friendly / expert / fun]

For each post include:
1. The platform (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn)
2. The hook (first line — must stop the scroll)
3. The body copy (2-4 sentences)
4. A CTA
5. 5 relevant hashtags

Mix of post types: educational tip, behind the scenes, customer result/testimonial, promotional, and community/local.
```

---

## Prompt 2 — Before & After Post

```
Write a social media post for a [BUSINESS TYPE] showcasing a before-and-after result.

Details:
- Service provided: [SERVICE]
- Key result or transformation: [DESCRIBE THE CHANGE]
- Customer location (optional): [SUBURB/CITY]

The post should:
- Open with a compelling hook
- Describe the transformation without being boastful
- Include a subtle CTA (DM us, call us, link in bio)
- Be suitable for Facebook and Instagram

Add 5 relevant local hashtags at the end.
```

---

## Prompt 3 — Educational "Did You Know" Post

```
Write an educational social media post for a [BUSINESS TYPE] that teaches the audience something useful and positions us as experts.

Topic: [e.g. "when to replace your hot water system" / "3 signs your accounts need a review" / "why your gym results have stalled"]
Audience: [TARGET CUSTOMER]
Platform: [Facebook / Instagram / LinkedIn]

The post should:
- Open with a surprising stat or question
- Give 3 practical tips or facts
- End with a soft CTA
- Be under 200 words
- Sound like a trusted expert, not a salesperson
```

---

## Prompt 4 — Local Community Post

```
Write a social media post for a [BUSINESS TYPE] in [LOCATION] that connects with the local community.

Angle: [choose one]
- Celebrating a local event or milestone
- Giving a shoutout to another local business
- Commenting on something happening in the local area
- Sharing pride in being a local business

Tone: warm, community-focused, genuine
Platform: Facebook

This post is NOT about selling. It's about building local presence and trust.
```

---

## Prompt 5 — Testimonial / Social Proof Post

```
Turn this customer review or testimonial into a social media post:

Review text: [PASTE REVIEW]
Customer name: [FIRST NAME ONLY or initials]
Service they received: [SERVICE]

The post should:
- Lead with the most powerful line from the review
- Give brief context on what we did
- End with a CTA for others to book/enquire
- Feel authentic, not over-produced
- Be suitable for Facebook and Instagram

Add 5 relevant local hashtags.
```

---

## Prompt 6 — Promotional Post (Offer or Seasonal)

```
Write a promotional social media post for a [BUSINESS TYPE] announcing [OFFER/PROMOTION].

Offer details:
- What: [DESCRIBE OFFER]
- Who it's for: [TARGET CUSTOMER]
- Expiry: [DATE]
- How to claim: [e.g. call, DM, use code X]

The post should:
- Create urgency without being pushy
- Focus on the benefit, not just the discount
- Be direct with the CTA
- Suit Facebook and Instagram
- Be under 150 words

Add urgency language naturally — do not use "Don't miss out" or "Act now" (overused).
```

---

## Local Business Content Strategy (30-Second Summary)

For AI systems and Google to see you as a trusted local authority, post consistently on at least one platform:

| Post Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Educational tip | 1-2x/week | Builds trust and expertise |
| Customer result | 1x/week | Social proof |
| Behind the scenes | 1x/week | Humanises the brand |
| Local/community | 1x/fortnight | Local relevance signals |
| Promotional | 1-2x/month | Revenue driver |

**Consistency beats virality.** 3 solid posts a week for 6 months beats one viral post and then silence.
