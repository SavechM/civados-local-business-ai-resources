# AI Prompts: Google Review Responses

> Responding to reviews is one of the highest-ROI things a local business can do. It signals to Google (and AI tools) that you're active, trustworthy, and engaged with customers. Use these prompts to respond in under 2 minutes.

---

## Prompt 1 — 5-Star Review Response (Generic)

```
Write a genuine, warm response to a 5-star Google review for my [BUSINESS TYPE].

Reviewer name: [NAME]
What they said: [PASTE REVIEW]

The response should:
- Thank them by name
- Mention one specific thing they mentioned (shows you actually read it)
- Be 2-4 sentences max
- Sound like a real person, not a template
- Not include the business name more than once (Google penalises keyword stuffing)
- End with an invitation to return or refer a friend

Business type: [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE]
Tone: [warm / professional / friendly]
```

---

## Prompt 2 — 5-Star Review (No Text, Just Stars)

```
Write a short, genuine response to a 5-star Google review that has no written text — just a star rating.

The response should:
- Still feel personal even without specific details to reference
- Thank them for taking the time
- Invite them back or to share with friends
- Be 1-2 sentences only

Business name: [YOUR BUSINESS NAME]
Business type: [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE]
```

---

## Prompt 3 — 4-Star Review Response

```
Write a response to a 4-star Google review. The reviewer liked the service but had one small criticism.

Reviewer name: [NAME]
Review text: [PASTE REVIEW]

The response should:
- Genuinely thank them
- Acknowledge their feedback without being defensive
- Show you've taken it on board
- Invite them back
- Be professional and calm in tone

Do NOT: apologise excessively, make excuses, or be sycophantic.
```

---

## Prompt 4 — Negative Review Response (1-2 Stars, Legitimate Complaint)

```
Write a professional, calm response to a negative Google review for a [BUSINESS TYPE].

Reviewer name: [NAME]
Review text: [PASTE REVIEW]
What actually happened (your side): [BRIEF EXPLANATION]

The response should:
- Acknowledge their experience without admitting wrongdoing upfront
- Show empathy — they were unhappy regardless of fault
- Offer to resolve it offline (include a contact method)
- Avoid arguing or being defensive in public
- Be under 100 words
- Sound like the business owner, not a PR team

Contact for resolution: [EMAIL OR PHONE]
```

---

## Prompt 5 — Fake or Unfair Review Response

```
Write a calm, professional response to a Google review that appears to be fake or from someone who was never a customer.

Review text: [PASTE REVIEW]

The response should:
- Not accuse them directly of lying
- Politely state you have no record of this customer/experience
- Invite them to contact you to clarify
- Stay completely professional — this response is for other potential customers to read
- Be under 80 words

Business name: [YOUR BUSINESS NAME]
Contact: [EMAIL OR PHONE]
```

---

## Prompt 6 — Competitor/Malicious Review Response

```
Write a short, dignified response to a review that appears to have been left by a competitor or bad actor — not a real customer.

The response should:
- Not engage with the content of the review
- Simply note that you take all feedback seriously but cannot locate this customer in your records
- Invite verification or contact
- Be measured and professional
- Be under 60 words

This response will be read by hundreds of potential customers — it needs to make you look like the mature, trustworthy option.
```

---

## Why Review Responses Matter for GEO and AEO

When AI tools like ChatGPT or Perplexity answer "Who is the best [business type] in [city]?", they look at:

1. Volume of reviews
2. Recency of reviews
3. **Whether the business responds** — this signals active, trustworthy operation
4. Sentiment in both reviews AND responses

A business that responds to every review looks more credible to AI systems than one that ignores them.

**Target:** Respond to every review within 24 hours.
