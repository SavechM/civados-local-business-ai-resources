# AI Prompts: Email Campaigns for Local Businesses

> Use these prompts to generate email campaigns in minutes. Replace [BRACKETS] with your details.

---

## Prompt 1 — Seasonal Promotion Email

```
Write a promotional email for a [BUSINESS TYPE] for [SEASON/OCCASION].

Business details:
- Business name: [NAME]
- Service being promoted: [SERVICE]
- Offer: [DISCOUNT / BONUS / PACKAGE]
- Offer expiry: [DATE]
- CTA: [Call / Book online / Reply to this email]

The email should:
- Have a subject line that gets opened (give me 3 options)
- Open with a hook relevant to the season
- Make the offer crystal clear in the first 3 sentences
- Use urgency without being pushy
- Be under 200 words
- Have one CTA only

Tone: [friendly / professional / direct]
```

## Prompt 2 — Re-Engagement Email (Dormant Customers)

```
Write a re-engagement email for a [BUSINESS TYPE] targeting customers who haven't purchased or booked in [TIMEFRAME].

Business name: [NAME]
Last service they used: [SERVICE or "unknown"]
Goal: Get them to rebook or enquire

The email should:
- Acknowledge the time that's passed naturally (don't make it awkward)
- Remind them of the value we provided
- Offer an incentive to come back (optional — include if we have one: [OFFER or "none"])
- Be conversational and human
- Include one clear CTA
- Have 3 subject line options

The tone should feel like a message from a trusted local business, not a faceless corporation.
```

## Prompt 3 — Referral Request Email

```
Write an email asking happy customers for referrals.

Business name: [NAME]
Business type: [TYPE]
Referral incentive (if any): [e.g. "£25 off their next service" / "none"]
Who to refer: [e.g. friends, family, colleagues]

The email should:
- Feel genuine, not transactional
- Thank them for being a customer first
- Make the referral ask clear but not awkward
- Explain how easy it is to refer (e.g. just forward this email / share our number)
- Include a CTA

Give me 3 subject line options.
```

## Prompt 4 — Post-Job Follow-Up Email

```
Write a post-job follow-up email sent to a customer 24-48 hours after completing their service.

Business name: [NAME]
Business type: [TYPE]
Customer name: [FIRST NAME] (or use {{contact.first_name}} for GHL)
Service completed: [SERVICE]

The email should:
- Thank them for choosing us
- Check in on satisfaction briefly
- Include a review request with a direct Google link: [YOUR GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]
- Mention referrals lightly
- Be short — under 150 words
- Feel personal, not automated (even if it is)

Give me 3 subject line options.
```

## Prompt 5 — New Service Announcement Email

```
Write an email announcing a new service to existing customers.

Business name: [NAME]
New service: [SERVICE NAME]
What it does / the problem it solves: [DESCRIPTION]
Who it's for: [TARGET CUSTOMER TYPE]
Special launch offer (if any): [OFFER or "none"]

The email should:
- Lead with the problem it solves, not the service name
- Be under 200 words
- Have a clear CTA (book, enquire, call)
- Feel like an announcement from a trusted business, not a sales pitch

Give me 3 subject line options.
```

## Subject Line Formulas (Copy These Directly)

These formulas reliably get high open rates for local businesses:

**Curiosity:**
- `[FIRST NAME], have you tried this?`
- `Something we've been working on for you`
- `Quick question, [FIRST NAME]`

**Direct/Offer:**
- `[OFFER] — expires [DATE]`
- `[DISCOUNT]% off [SERVICE] this [MONTH]`
- `Your [SEASON] deal from [BUSINESS NAME]`

**Re-engagement:**
- `It's been a while, [FIRST NAME]`
- `We miss you at [BUSINESS NAME]`
- `Still thinking about [SERVICE]?`

**Social proof:**
- `[NUMBER] customers loved this — your turn`
- `Why [SUBURB] locals choose us for [SERVICE]`
