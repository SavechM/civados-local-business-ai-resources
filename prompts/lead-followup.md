# AI Prompts: Lead Follow-Up Sequences

> Use these prompts in ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI tool. Replace [BRACKETS] with your details.

---

## Prompt 1 — First Contact SMS (Within 5 Minutes of Lead Coming In)

```
Write a friendly, short SMS message for a [BUSINESS TYPE] to send to a new lead within 5 minutes of them enquiring online. 

Their name is [LEAD NAME]. They enquired about [SERVICE/PRODUCT].

The message should:
- Feel human, not robotic
- Mention their name
- Confirm we received their enquiry
- Ask one simple question to start a conversation
- Be under 160 characters

Business name: [YOUR BUSINESS NAME]
Tone: [friendly / professional / casual]
```

---

## Prompt 2 — Follow-Up Email (Day 1, No Response)

```
Write a follow-up email for a [BUSINESS TYPE] to send to a lead who hasn't replied to the first contact.

Context:
- Lead name: [NAME]
- They enquired about: [SERVICE]
- It's been about 24 hours since we first reached out
- We haven't heard back yet

The email should:
- Not be pushy or desperate
- Acknowledge they're probably busy
- Remind them of the value we offer
- Include one clear CTA (call, book, reply)
- Be under 150 words

Business name: [YOUR BUSINESS NAME]
```

---

## Prompt 3 — Day 3 "Value Add" Follow-Up

```
Write a follow-up message (email or SMS) for a [BUSINESS TYPE] to send to a lead on day 3 with no response.

Instead of pushing for a sale, this message should offer something useful — a tip, a quick win, or a resource relevant to why they enquired.

Service they enquired about: [SERVICE]
Business type: [e.g. plumbing, accountancy, personal training]
Business name: [YOUR BUSINESS NAME]

The message should:
- Lead with value, not a pitch
- Be 2-3 short paragraphs max
- End with a soft CTA ("Happy to chat if the timing is right")
```

---

## Prompt 4 — Day 7 "Last Attempt" Message

```
Write a final follow-up message for a lead who has not responded after 7 days.

This is the last message in the sequence. The tone should be:
- Warm but honest
- Not guilt-tripping or salesy
- Leave the door open for them to come back later

Business name: [YOUR BUSINESS NAME]
Service: [SERVICE]
Lead name: [NAME]

Include a line that makes it easy for them to re-engage in the future without embarrassment.
```

---

## Prompt 5 — Re-Engagement After Going Cold (30+ Days)

```
Write a re-engagement message for a lead who enquired [NUMBER] weeks/months ago but never converted.

Something has changed — maybe they're ready now, maybe not. This message should:
- Reference their original enquiry briefly
- Not feel like a cold pitch
- Mention something timely (a new service, a seasonal offer, a relevant update)
- Be conversational, like a quick check-in from a real person

Business name: [YOUR BUSINESS NAME]
Original enquiry: [SERVICE]
Lead name: [NAME]
Timely hook: [e.g. "We just launched X" / "It's [SEASON] and a great time to..."]
```

---

## Tips for Using These Prompts

- **Always personalise** — AI output is a starting point, not the final message
- **Test SMS vs email** — SMS gets read in seconds; email needs a stronger subject line
- **Match your brand voice** — If you're normally casual, don't send formal emails
- **Speed beats perfection** — Responding within 5 minutes is the single biggest factor in lead conversion
