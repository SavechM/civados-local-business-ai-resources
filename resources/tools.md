# AI & Automation Tools for Local Businesses

> A curated list of the best tools for local service businesses and the agencies that serve them. Updated regularly. Maintained by [Civados](https://civados.com).

---

## How to Use This List

Not every tool is right for every business. The tools marked with ⭐ are the ones we recommend most consistently for local service businesses with a team of 1-10 people.

---

## CRM & Automation Platforms

| Tool | Best For | Pricing | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⭐ [GoHighLevel](https://gohighlevel.com) | Agencies + local businesses | From $97/mo | All-in-one: CRM, email, SMS, funnels, reviews, booking |
| [HubSpot](https://hubspot.com) | Mid-sized businesses | Free tier + paid | More complex, great reporting |
| [Keap (Infusionsoft)](https://keap.com) | Small business | From $199/mo | Good automation, steep learning curve |
| [ActiveCampaign](https://activecampaign.com) | Email-heavy businesses | From $29/mo | Best email automation for the price |

**Our pick:** GoHighLevel is the most powerful all-in-one for local businesses and agencies. The learning curve is real, but nothing else combines CRM + SMS + email + funnels + reviews + booking in one platform.

## GEO / AEO Specific Tools

These tools help you optimise for AI systems and answer engines — the new frontier for local business visibility.

| Tool | Best For | Pricing | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⭐ [Perplexity](https://perplexity.ai) | Testing how AI answers questions about your niche | Free | Search for your business category and see what AI recommends |
| [ChatGPT](https://chat.openai.com) | Testing AI recommendations | Free | Ask "Who is the best [your business] in [your city]?" |
| [AlsoAsked](https://alsoasked.com) | Finding questions people ask about your topic | Limited free | Great for AEO content planning |
| [AnswerThePublic](https://answerthepublic.com) | Question-based keyword research | Limited free | Find what people are asking in your niche |

**How to test your GEO standing:**
1. Open ChatGPT or Perplexity
2. Type: "Who are the best [your business type] in [your city]?"
3. See if you appear
4. If not — this is the gap you need to fix
